01 Now building

In progress

Catch.

An app for keeping up with your friendships.

Friendships rarely end. They drift. Life gets busy, months go by, and people you care about quietly slip out of orbit. Catch helps you keep track of your friendships so you stay close to the people who matter, before "we should catch up" turns into "it's been years."

02 Selected work

Things I've built.

Four very different problems, one habit: find where everyday life gets complicated and build something that makes it lighter.

Fintech · Mexico ⇄ US

Adulting

A personal finance app for people whose lives span Mexico and the US. Two countries can mean two currencies, two banking systems and twice the paperwork. Adulting is built to make money across the border make sense.

AI interpretability · Non-profit

Azetta AI

A non-profit focused on AI interpretability: understanding what's actually happening inside AI models. The systems starting to shape our lives shouldn't be black boxes, and making them legible is how we learn to trust them.

Travel · Gamified planning

Moonshine of the Seas

A gamified trip-planning tool. It turns the to-dos, decisions and logistics of planning a trip into a game people actually want to play, so the planning becomes part of the adventure.

Civic tech · Citizen engagement

SIVICO

A citizen engagement app that makes it easier for people to take part in the civic life of their communities: to stay informed, be heard and participate, without having to fight the process to do it.

03 About

A little about me.

I'm drawn to the places where everyday life gets complicated, and where good software can make it a little lighter.

My projects have taken me from cross-border personal finance to civic participation, from making trip planning fun to helping people understand how AI works. Different domains, same question: how do you build tools that actually show up for people?

Right now I'm focused on Catch, because the people in our lives are the most important thing we have to keep track of.
